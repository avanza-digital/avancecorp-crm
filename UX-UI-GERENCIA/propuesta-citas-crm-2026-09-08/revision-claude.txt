VERDICT:
CHANGES_REQUESTED

SUMMARY:
The month/week wrapper, the unique-lead average and the explicit `citaAnteriorId` linkage are conceptually sound and correctly scoped as a local prototype: no writes, no RPCs, no auth surface, and the model reuse via `consultarCitas` keeps a single filtering path for all three views. The defects I can evidence are: (1) an unguarded `promedio === null` reaching the always-rendered KPI strip on a reachable empty-cohort path, (2) a navigation path (`verAnalista`) that does not reset filters even though its sibling `verAgenda` does, precisely for out-of-filter follow-up appointments, (3) a per-advisor `Leads con cita` column whose rows cannot sum to the footer, unexplained in the on-screen note, and (4) week-range tests that can pass vacuously and therefore do not actually prove the four tramos cover the month. Several `periodo: 'custom'` branches in `filtros.tsx` are now unreachable and silently ignored by `consultarCitas`.

[P2] The `Leads con cita` column cannot sum to its own footer, and the note only explains the average
File:
CRM-Avance-Corp/app/src/prototypes/citas-crm/resultados.tsx
Lines:
53 (tfoot), 55; datos.ts:48-55
Problem:
Per-advisor `leads`/`repetidos` come from `resumenLeads(citas.filter(c => c.analista === persona.id))` (`datos.ts:54`), while the footer uses `resumenLeads(citas)` over globally distinct ids. A lead attended by two advisors is counted once in the footer and once per advisor.
Evidence:
`datos.test.ts:17-26` deliberately constructs this case: `lead-a` has appointments with `ana` and `diego`; per-advisor leads are 1 and 2 (sum 3) while `resumenLeads(citas).leads` is 2. `CITAS_CRM` (`datos.ts:22-29`) rewrites only `nombre/telefono/origen/monto/moneda` and never `analista`, so repeated indices such as `24: 0` and `36: 0` can legitimately map appointments of different advisors onto the same `L-001`.
Impact:
In the same table, `Citas` sums to the footer but `Leads con cita` and `Leads con 2+ citas` do not. The on-screen note (`resultados.tsx:55`) explains only that the average is not an average of averages, so a manager auditing the column will read it as an arithmetic error.
Recommendation:
Extend the note to state that a lead attended by more than one advisor counts once in the total and once per advisor, and consider marking the footer cells for those two columns (e.g. "26 únicos") so the non-additive columns are visually distinguishable.
