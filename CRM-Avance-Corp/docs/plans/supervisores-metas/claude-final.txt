SUMMARY:
The SQL change is correct and narrowly scoped. Three things hold up:

- **Global readers unchanged.** The global branch is untouched, and the parity test covers it byte for byte.
- **Supervisor sees only their own row.** In open months the UUID filter is applied before any join, so a supervisor's `fuera_ranking` contains only their own row. Closed months filter the frozen photo by `persona_id`, `rol_crm` and `motivo`, without recomputing or writing anything.
- **No cross-team exposure found.** I found no way for a supervisor to see another supervisor's or another team's data in the attached code.

The frontend adapter adds own sales once to the team and once to the company, and keeps the analysts' conversion target. Two findings need changes or evidence before promotion:

- **(P2-1)** From October, the frontend moves supervisor rows out of `fueraRanking` into a new optional `supervisores` field. Because the field is optional, the compiler cannot catch a consumer that still reads only `fueraRanking`.
- **(P2-2)** Management's per-supervisor team views may not include the supervisor's own sales, while the supervisor's own view does.

The remaining items are P3 or explicit scope limits.
